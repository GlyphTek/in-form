# IN-FORM showcase video: script and shot list

Target length: about 90 seconds to 2 minutes. Record the **real app**: no mock data,
no sped-up loading. If something is slow, leave it; the honesty is the point.

## Before you record

- Use the Windows download, started with `start.bat`, so the video shows what people get.
- Start clean: delete `%APPDATA%\IN-FORM` (close the app first) so the setup screen is empty.
- Use a fresh browser window with no other tabs or bookmarks showing. A window about 1280 x 800 reads well.
- Zoom the browser to 110 to 125 percent so the text is readable on a phone.
- Close anything private on screen. Do a dry run once; the news will differ every day, so do not script headlines.
- Record the screen and your voice together, or record the screen first and add a voiceover after.

## Shot list

| # | Time | On screen | Say (adapt freely) |

| 6 | 0:42 | Press START IN-FORM | "Then press start." |

## Optional extra (about 15 seconds): the honest failure

Turn off Wi-Fi, press PRESS, and show the message that live signals are unavailable
and that nothing is shown rather than placeholder content. Say: "If it can't reach
your sources, it tells you. It never makes things up." Turn Wi-Fi back on afterwards.

## Things not to claim

- Do not call it AI. It is keyword matching plus simple learning.
- Do not promise Mac or Linux downloads; say they run from source.
- Do not say "private" without saying why: data stays on your computer, and the news
  searches go to Google News.
- Say "free", not "open source", until a licence is chosen.

## After recording

Trim the start and end only. Add captions if you can. Upload it, then put the link at
the top of the README and in the release notes.
